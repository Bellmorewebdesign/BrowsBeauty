## Important sales/pricing wording

Avoid promising “no fees per sale” without qualification. A safer claim is:

> No additional website commission on each booking. Standard payment-processing fees would still apply if online payments are added.

For the mockup, booking controls should be clearly nonfunctional or display a message such as **Demo only — booking is not submitted**.
